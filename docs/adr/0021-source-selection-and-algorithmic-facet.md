# ADR 0021: Algorithmic selection and historical LGWT freeze

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-10
- Basis: accepted foundation-next product decisions

Algorithmic behavior is eligible when prerequisites, invariants and useful Go
practice are explicit. Preserve activity implementation/test-writing/debugging
and existing skills. A future optional task-facet sidecar may display an
algorithmic badge by stable task ID; adding it requires a bounded tooling batch
before use. This ADR does not change task.schema.json or reinterpret activity.

Retain every historical Exercism rejection. Append reconsideration evidence
(old decision, pedagogical disposition, source/commit/path, remaining license
or lineage restriction, reviewer). A change in teaching policy does not approve
canonical problem-specifications descriptions/generated cases. Those remain
reference-only, including indirect copying through the track repository.

Freeze Learn Go with Tests for new selection and theory. Keep its registry entry
and the 16 already accepted adaptations with their exact historical bindings.
Do not change the entire source to reference-only, which would invalidate
history. New source bindings, expansion of historical artifact scope, and new
LGWT-derived tasks are rejected during source/content review. A mechanical
freeze guard, if introduced, snapshots the existing binding identities and rejects
additions while permitting unchanged historical validation; it belongs to a
separate tooling batch with fixtures. Existing task solutions/tests are preserved.

No migration is needed: historical reviews, task IDs, source records and content
remain intact. New inventory entries are not ready exercises. P3 decides bounded
artifact permissions with hashes, notices and lineage before P5 authors any task.
Rollback never deletes historical evidence or weakens reference-only policy.
